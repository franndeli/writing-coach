# Writing workspace

This is a private **writing-coach** workspace. For anything about writing, posts, the journal, goals or style,
load the `writing-coach` skill and follow its rules. They're in the skill's `references/rules.md`.

The core rule: **the words are always mine.** Restructure, cut and question, but never add words of your own.
