# My writing style

> Example profile for the fictional writer Alex Rivera. A real one is generated from 10–20 samples.

## Voice & tone
- **Plain, slightly self-deprecating, no jargon.** `samples/linkedin/2025-11-04-first-team.md`: "I thought my job was to have answers."

## Rhythm & sentence shape
- **Short sentences, one per line, with a punchy fragment to land the point.** Same sample: "Nobody knew. So we stopped doing it."

## Structure
### How I open
- With a confession or a blunt claim. "A year ago I became a manager and I thought my job was to have answers."
### How I close
- With a short action or consequence, not a question to the audience. "So we stopped doing it."

## Words & phrases I'd never use
- "Thrilled to announce", "game-changer", "synergy" *(inferred: absent from all samples)*
