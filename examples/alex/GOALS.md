# My account goals

## Why I post
To think out loud about running engineering teams, and to meet other managers who are figuring out the same things.

## Audience
Engineering managers and tech leads at small and mid-size companies.

## Topics I post about
- Team process that actually changed something (meetings, planning, on-call)
- Lessons from mistakes I made as a manager
- Hiring and onboarding

## Topics I never post about
- Anything that identifies a report's performance or personal life
- My employer's numbers or roadmap
- Hot takes on AI replacing engineers

## Per platform

| | LinkedIn | Blog |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | Short experiments and lessons, one idea per post | Longer write-ups when an experiment runs for a quarter |
| Cadence I'm comfortable with | 1–2 a month, fine to skip a month | A few a year |

## What success looks like
A manager I don't know replies with their own version of the experiment.
